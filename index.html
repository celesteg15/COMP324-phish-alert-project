How scoring works & how to WIN

Points each question: you start from a base of +100 if your answer is correct, or 0 if it is not. Your score for that round cannot go below zero.

  • Hint: opening the hint before you submit costs −25 points on that question.
  • Timer: each message uses its own difficulty timer (Easy 60s, Medium 45s, Hard 30s). Submitting after time runs out costs −40 points on that question.

How to “win”: finish the quiz with a high correct count and a high point total by answering correctly, staying under the timer, and avoiding hints when you can. After each submit, a pop-up shows exactly how many points you earned on that question (for example +100, +35, or 0).
